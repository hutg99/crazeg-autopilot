Privacy Policy — CrazeG Autopilot

Effective Date: November 16, 2026
Operator: Craze G (@crazegzero)

1. Overview

CrazeG Autopilot is a private, single-user automation tool used exclusively by Craze G to upload vertical video drafts to @crazegzero's TikTok Inbox.

2. Data Accessed & Stored

  • OAuth Credentials: Temporary OAuth2 access and refresh tokens (user.info.basic, video.upload) are stored locally on the operator's private workstation in .env solely to authenticate uploads to @crazegzero.
  • Media Files: Local video files processed on the operator's machine are transmitted directly to TikTok's official API.
  • No Third-Party Users: This application does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from any third-party users.

3. Data Retention & Revocation

OAuth tokens can be revoked at any time from TikTok's Security & Permissions settings or by deleting the local .env file.

4. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact Craze G via @crazegzero on TikTok.
